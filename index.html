ABOUT US

Creating Change, One Community at a Time

Sarvyahith is a non-profit organisation dedicated to serving society through compassion, inclusion, and collective welfare. The name Sarvyahith is derived from two Sanskrit words: "Sarv" (सर्व) meaning all, everyone, or universal, and "Hit" (हित) meaning welfare, well-being, or benefit. Together, the name reflects a timeless philosophy of working for the good of all, promoting harmony, unity, and actions that uplift the entire community rather than a select few. At Sarvyahith, we believe that true change begins with empathy and shared responsibility. More than just a name, Sarvyahith is a commitment to ensuring that no call for help goes unheard. Our mission is to stand beside those in need—not through charity alone, but through genuine human connection, dignity, and empowerment. Every initiative we undertake is guided by the belief that care should never be conditional and that every individual deserves respect, opportunity, and support. Together, we strive to build a society where every life is valued, every hand is uplifted, and every voice is heard.

Our Mission

Empower individuals and communities through meaningful opportunities and support.

Our Vision

A compassionate and inclusive society where everyone has equal opportunities to thrive.

OUR PROJECTS

Creating Impact Through Action

Every initiative brings us one step closer to a stronger, healthier and more inclusive community.

Gaushala Drive

Sarvyahith's first-ever on-ground drive was organized at a gaushala, where our team came together to serve and care for the cows. During the drive, we fed 50 cows, spent quality time with them, and experienced the joy of serving them with kindness and compassion. The initiative highlighted the importance of teamwork, empathy, and selfless service, showing us how even small acts of care can create a meaningful impact on the lives of others while strengthening our sense of community and responsibility.

Project Pehchaan Fundraiser Food Stall

A fundraiser food stall was organized at Swami Shraddhanand College, University of Delhi, in support of Project Pehchaan. Our team managed the stall, served food, interacted with visitors, and shared the mission of Project Pehchaan and how it works towards restoring the identity and confidence of acid attack survivors. Every purchase contributed to the fundraiser, helping us raise funds while also spreading awareness and encouraging more people to become a part of the cause.
